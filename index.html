Straight answers

What people ask before they book

Will you tell me if solar is not worth it on my house?

Yes. A shaded or awkward roof is a bad job for you and for us. We would rather say so.

My roof is old. Should I do that first?

Usually yes — it is far cheaper than taking an array off and putting it back. Tell us on the form and we will be straight with you.

Do you do the work yourselves?

Yes. Our own crew, with the owner on site.

What does it cost?

It depends on the system size your roof and your bill call for. You get a written proposal with the full cost before you commit. (Concept note: Gallagher Solar to confirm any finance or payment options here.)
